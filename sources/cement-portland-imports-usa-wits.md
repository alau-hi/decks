# Portland cement (HS 252329) exports to the USA — 2024 vs 2025 (WITS / UN Comtrade)

Recorded 2026-09-01. Live-page reads. Companion to
[cement-clinker-imports-2023-wits.md](cement-clinker-imports-2023-wits.md) (HS 252310)
and the OEC HS4 2523 figures in claims.yaml. Three different product bases — never mix.

Values are partner-reported (mirror) customs values, essentially FOB — Turkish seaborne
freight to the US is not in these unit values, so the landed-cost gap vs Canadian
truck/rail cement is narrower than the ratio below.

| Exporter to USA | 2024 value | 2024 volume | 2025 value | 2025 volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turkey | $386.0M | 7.00M t | $422.6M | 7.27M t |
| Canada | $427.9M | 3.59M t | $390.0M | 3.56M t |
| European Union | $153.9M | 2.53M t | $93.6M | 1.52M t |
| Greece | $100.8M | 1.83M t | $66.0M | 1.31M t |
| Mexico | $98.6M | 1.10M t | $59.0M | 0.77M t |

Derived unit values (value ÷ volume):

| | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Turkey | ~$55/t | ~$58/t |
| Canada | ~$119/t | ~$110/t |

Key readings:
- **By tonnage Turkey has led Canada ~2:1 in both years** on this portland line; by
  value Canada led in 2024 and Turkey took the lead in 2025. The OEC HS4 2523 totals
  (all cement lines) still show Canada first by value in both years because Canadian
  cement carries ~2x the unit value.
- Share of US apparent consumption (~114M t/yr, derived from the USGS 22% reliance /
  ~25M t imports register [conf: M]): Turkey ~6.1% (2024) → ~6.4% (2025); Canada ~3.1%
  both years.

## Freight from Turkey (recorded 2026-09-01)

- Platts Cement Weekly (2026-01-15): **Mediterranean -> Houston cement freight $26.00/mt**
  (40kt vessel). Platts began weekly Turkey cement + freight-to-North-America
  assessments in Jan 2025.
- Same date: OPC (CEM I 42.5R) bulk **FOB Turkey $54.00/mt**; Medcem 2026 contracts
  $53.5-55/mt FOB (2025: $52/mt).
- Trade commentary puts the ocean leg at ~$15-20/t in softer freight markets [conf: M].
- Cross-check vs the WITS unit values above: Turkey ~$58/t FOB + ~$26/t freight ≈
  **~$84/t landed**, still well under Canada's ~$110/t unit value - the landed gap is
  ~$25-30/t, not the 2:1 the FOB ratio suggests.
