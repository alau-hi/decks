# Cement clinker exports to the USA, 2023 (WITS / UN Comtrade)

Recorded 2026-09-01 at Alex's request ("keep handy"). Retrieved from the live WITS page.

- **Product**: HS 252310 — **cement clinkers** (the kiln intermediate). This is a
  *subset* of HS4 2523 "Cement", the basis of the OEC figures in the Gap slide's cement
  tooltip (2025: Canada $514M, Turkey $485M, Vietnam $207M — see claims.yaml). Do not mix
  the two bases: clinker trade is ~2 orders of magnitude smaller than finished cement.
- **Source**: [WITS — Comtrade, exports to USA, 2023, HS 252310](https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/All/year/2023/tradeflow/Exports/partner/USA/product/252310)
- Values are partner-reported exports to the USA (mirror data), in USD thousands.
- WITS lists the European Union as an aggregate row *alongside* its member states
  (France, Greece, Poland, Spain, Croatia, Sweden also appear individually) — do not sum
  the EU row with member rows.
