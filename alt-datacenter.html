The trade-off was tone against legibility — the warmer the frame, the less obviously it said "server racks" — and 1–5 all sat somewhere on that line. 6 and 7 get off it: keep the cabinets black, and make only the lights warm. A data centre is a lot of black boxes; the fix was never to warm the boxes up until they looked like rust, it was to match the band on light instead of on hue.

6 is in the deck now, with 7 as the runner-up. Of the original five: 2 was where I landed — warm enough to belong beside the potline, with enough rack face and cabling to be read correctly at the size it actually runs. 1 is the alternative: the best tonal match of the five and the better frame if the band reading as one image matters more than the panel reading on its own. 3 I would drop — it looked promising full-size and did not survive the crop.
