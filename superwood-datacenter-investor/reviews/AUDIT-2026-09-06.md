# Technical audit — slides.html (Impeccable audit, 2026-09-06)

Score 15/20 (Good). Accessibility 3, Performance 3, Responsive 2, Theming 3, Implementation integrity 4.
Measured in the local preview at 1400x850 and 375x812; detector run once; nothing fixed in this pass.

## Findings by severity

- [P1] Slide 6 overflows horizontally on phones (section scrollWidth 474 px at a 375 px viewport). The desktop grid
  override `.hbars.det .hb{grid-template-columns:11rem minmax(0,1fr) 6.8rem 7rem}` outranks the phone rule
  `.hb{grid-template-columns:1fr}`, so the bar track collapses to 0 px and the value and bubble columns spill right.
  Fix: scope the override to `(min-width:901px)` or repeat the phone rule with the same specificity. → /impeccable adapt
- [P1] Touch targets: dock buttons are 8x8 px dots on phones, prev/next are 28x19 px, the mass/carbon tabs 22 px tall.
  WCAG 2.5.8 wants 24 px minimum, 44 px is the comfortable target. Fix: padded hit areas around the dots and buttons,
  taller tabs under `(pointer:coarse)`. → /impeccable adapt
- [P2] Text below the deck's own 11 px floor: footnotes render at 9 px, chart axis labels and bubble-column headers at
  10 px. Contrast is fine (5.6:1 muted, 7.6:1 kicker) but the size is below comfortable reading on a laptop.
  → /impeccable typeset
- [P2] Slide 15 pop-ups live in CSS `::after` content, which screen readers do not announce, and the focusable wrapper has
  no `aria-describedby`. Fix: render the tip as a real element referenced by `aria-describedby`, toggled by hover/focus.
  → /impeccable harden
- [P2] First paint carries `cover_bg.jpg` at 704 KB and `campus_hero.jpg` at 476 KB. Fix: WebP at ~1600 px wide, or
  `srcset` for phones. → /impeccable optimize
- [P3] 20 unreferenced files ship in `assets/` (about 6 MB, `boards.png` 1.1 MB, `structure.png` 676 KB, `beam_steel.png`
  520 KB, `app_shell.jpg` 420 KB). No runtime cost, only deploy weight; move to `prep/` or delete. → /impeccable optimize
- [P3] Chart material colors (`#8c8478`, `#5a4a36`, `#3a2b1a`) are literals in the chart builder rather than tokens; the deck
  is single-theme by design, so no dark/light break. → /impeccable document
- [P3] 340 inline `style=""` attributes and no `<main>` landmark. Slides are `<section>`s under a scrolling deck, which
  reads acceptably; a `<main>` wrapper is a one-line gain. → /impeccable harden
- [P3] Detector: 6 "all-caps body" and 3 "cramped padding" warnings. Verified as false positives: the uppercase runs are
  kicker labels of 31–34 characters and the flush children are chart axis ticks against their baseline rule.

## Working well
- Token discipline: 228 `var(--…)` uses, 12 palette hexes, one visual world.
- Motion: `prefers-reduced-motion` disables reveals and smooth scroll while keeping state.
- Keyboard: arrow, digit, Home/End jumps, focus follows the slide, non-current slides `inert`, buffered presses, tablist
  with proper roles and arrow handling.
- Images: 31 of 45 lazy-loaded; decorative tiles carry empty alt with visible captions; project photos carry descriptive alt.
- No console errors; no horizontal scroll on desktop.
