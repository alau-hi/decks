mistaken for another company's deck: the token palette is a committed warm-wood world, the
citation-tooltip pattern is real and carries genuine sources, the charts are hand-built SVG

## Detailed Findings by Severity

### [P0] Mobile zoom collapse renders slide content illegible

- **Location**: `slides.html:1231–1275` (`fitSlides`), effects across `#s7`, `#gnet`, `#s8`,
  `#ask`, `#s18`, `#s19`
- **Category**: Responsive / Accessibility
- **Measured** at a 386px viewport with the root font-size set to the coarse-pointer branch
  (11.5px), after two `fitSlides()` passes:

  | Slide | zoom | elements <11px | elements <9px | smallest |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `#s7` Team | **0.54** | 34 | 32 | **4.0px** |
  | `#gnet` The Fleet | 0.82 | 51 | 49 | 5.8px |
  | `#s8` Investors | 0.96 | 22 | 11 | 7.7px |
  | `#ask` The Opportunity | 1.13 | 11 | 7 | 8.0px |
  | `#s18` Financing | 1.14 | 10 | 4 | 8.2px |
  | `#s19` Thank You | — | 2 | 2 | 8.3px |

- **Two independent causes**, both need fixing:
  1. `fitSlides()` has no legibility floor. The height loop clamps each step at `.75` and the
     width loop at `.6`, but those are *per-iteration* clamps across 8 iterations each — the
     product is effectively unbounded downward. On `#s7` the width-cap loop (l.1264–1273) is
     what drives it to 0.54, because the 2-column team grid plus 1-column advisors still
     overflows at zoom 1.
  2. The type ramp bottoms out at `.54–.7rem`. At the 11.5px mobile root floor that is
     6.2–8.0px *before any zoom* — which is why `#ask` shows 8px text at a zoom of 1.13.
     `.askterms span` is `.62rem`; `.gsub`, `.plab`, `.mill .sub`, `.step .num` and ~20 others
     sit in the same band.
- **Impact**: An investor opening this on a phone cannot read the team bios, the fleet
  economics, or the round terms. Those are the three slides that decide the meeting.
- **Verified visually**: screenshot at 390px confirms `#s7` renders its content in ~55% of the
  viewport height with a dead band below the advisors — the slide is simultaneously too small
  *and* not filling the space, which is the signature of the width loop overshooting.
- **Recommendation**: Give `fitSlides()` a hard floor (`z = Math.max(z, 0.8)`) and let slides
  that exceed it scroll internally on narrow viewports rather than shrink. Add a
  coarse-pointer/narrow-viewport branch that raises the small end of the type ramp (a
  `--fs-micro` token clamped to `max(11px, …)`) instead of relying on rem alone.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] Nav rail targets fail WCAG 2.2 AA target size

- **Location**: `slides.html:53–60`, `:636–637` (`nav.dots button` at ≤700px)
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Measured**: 13x12px per button, 16px pitch, 17 buttons stacked, at 386px. At 1440x900 they
  are 17.5px tall with a 6px gap.
- **WCAG**: 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), Level AA — requires 24x24 CSS px. Fails on mobile;
  marginal on desktop. Also fails 2.5.5 (AAA, 44x44) everywhere.
- **Impact**: On a phone the rail is effectively unusable — adjacent targets are 16px apart, so
  any tap is a coin flip between two slides.
- **Recommendation**: The `font-size:0` compaction at ≤700px is the right instinct (it keeps the
  dock narrow per the house rule), but the hit area must not shrink with the label. Keep the
  10px numeral and pad the button to a 24px minimum height with transparent padding.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] `fitSlides()` forces synchronous layout in a nested loop

- **Location**: `slides.html:1231–1275`
- **Category**: Performance
- **Detail**: Per section, up to 12 + 8 + 8 = 28 iterations, each writing `w.style.zoom` and
  then reading `offsetHeight` / `getBoundingClientRect()` — a forced reflow every pass. Worse,
  `lowest()` (l.1247) and `rightmost()` (l.1259) each call `w.querySelectorAll('*')` and then
  `getBoundingClientRect()` on **every descendant**, inside those 8-iteration loops. `#s7` has
  ~120 descendants, so one fit of that slide is on the order of 2000 forced reflows.
- **Triggers**: `load`, `fonts.ready`, again 600ms after load, every `resize` (150ms debounce),
  and once per lazy image that lands (l.1279–1280) — 17 lazy images.
- **Impact**: Measured 2.2s to `loadEventEnd` on a warm cache. On a mid-range phone the resize
  path (rotation, browser chrome collapsing on scroll) will visibly jank.
- **Recommendation**: Hoist the two `querySelectorAll('*')` calls out of their loops — the
  element list does not change between iterations, only the geometry. Batch reads before
  writes. Better: replace the iterative search with a single measure-then-solve pass, since
  `zoom` scales linearly and the required factor is computable in one read.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P1] Cover background ships as a 793 KB PNG

- **Location**: `slides.html:70`, `assets/supermill-two-aerial.png` (812,038 bytes)
- **Category**: Performance
- **Impact**: It is the LCP element of slide 1 — the first thing anyone sees. PNG is the wrong
  container for a photographic aerial render; at WebP q80 this is ~100–150 KB, an 80%+ cut on
  the deck's single heaviest byte cost. `cwebp` is available on this machine.
- **Note**: The rest of the image strategy is sound — portraits are already `.webp`, 17 images
  carry `loading="lazy"`. This one file is the outlier.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P1] Interactive cards are unlabeled divs

- **Location**: `slides.html:989`, `:994`, `:999`, `:1004`, `:1009` (`.person`), `:1017`,
  `:1021`, `:1025`, `:1029`, `:1033` (`.advc`)
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Detail**: Ten `<div tabindex="0">` elements that reveal a `.bio-pop` on `:hover` /
  `:focus-within`. No `role`, no accessible name, no `aria-expanded`, no programmatic
  association between the card and the popover it controls. The visible `.hint` ("Hover or tap
  for full bio") is presentational only.
- **Impact**: A screen-reader user tabs onto ten unnamed, unexplained stops. The bio content is
  reachable (it is in the DOM and `:focus-within` fires on tap) but is never announced as
  belonging to the card.
- **Recommendation**: `role="button"` + `aria-expanded` toggled on focus, and
  `aria-describedby` pointing at the `.bio-pop` id. The tap behavior already works; this is
  purely the missing semantic layer.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

1. **Zoom-to-fit substitutes for responsive design.** Breakpoints at 900/860/700/561px exist
   and do collapse grids correctly — but `fitSlides()` runs after them and is the mechanism
   that actually determines mobile legibility. A layout decision is being made by a numeric
   solver with no legibility constraint. Every mobile finding in this audit traces here.

2. **The type ramp has no floor.** Roughly 25 rules specify `.54rem`–`.70rem`. On desktop
   (root ~19px) those are 10–13px: fine. At the mobile root floor of 11.5px they are 6–8px
   before any zoom is applied. The ramp was designed for one root size.

3. **Inherited-deck drift.** ~40% of the stylesheet, both dead JS branches, and the duplicate
   rules all come from `aaron-deck` via the `README.md` derivation. Nothing pruned the
   stylesheet when the section list was cut.

4. **Per-instance inline overrides.** ~25 elements carry `style=` attributes doing layout work
   (`h2` font-size overrides, `max-width`, `margin-top`). The system is being overridden at
   each call site rather than extended with modifier classes — which is why `#s13 h2`,
   `#s8 h2`, `#dcn h2` and the inline `h2` sizes now form four competing sources of truth for
   one element's size.

## Positive Findings

Worth protecting:

- **Contrast is fully AA and was clearly deliberate.** Every token pair passes against `--ink`:
  cream 15.30:1, cream-dim 9.93:1, gold 9.70:1, wood-bright 7.59:1, muted 5.56:1, wood 5.17:1.
  All 40+ SVG chart labels pass on their own panel grounds. Zero confirmed contrast failures
  anywhere in the deck — rare, and it survived a light-on-dark panel inversion.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` is done properly** (l.109–115). It preserves final state rather
  than just killing transitions (`opacity:1!important;transform:none!important`), holds the
  background at its rest scale, and `jumpTo()` checks it before choosing smooth scroll. The
  `millup` animation is correctly scoped inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference)`.
- **`:focus-visible` is global** (l.61) with a real 2px outline and offset, not a reset.
- **Keyboard model is thorough**: arrows, PageUp/Down, Space, Home/End, plus digit-buffer jump
  with a 650ms commit and two-digit fast path. Modifier keys are correctly excluded (l.1218).
- **Print stylesheet is real** (l.707–717) — landscape letter, snap disabled, reveals forced
  visible, chrome hidden, `print-color-adjust:exact`.
- **Zero dependencies, one file, no build.** Nothing to audit for supply chain.
- **Every SVG chart carries a descriptive `role="img"` + `aria-label`** that states the actual
  trend, not just the chart type — l.850, l.923, l.1077. These are unusually good.
- **The comments are load-bearing and honest**, recording why a workaround exists rather than
  what a line does.
