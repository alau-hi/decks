## What makes it wondrous
- The thesis lands on a full-bleed image of a wooden server hall — the deck's one cinematic moment, labeled a concept rendering.
- Headlines carry the argument on their own: read only the eighteen headlines and the story is complete.
- The renders do the describing (fluted screen, fan wall, courtyard, decking, beams); captions are a few words.

## What keeps it grounded
- Every number keeps its source in a small footnote; nothing new was added.
- Horizons stay Immediate / Soon (1–3 yr) / Medium term / Long term (5+ yr), with the definitions strip on slide 9.
- Power, water and rates named as outside scope; carbon labeled pre-LCA; substitution factor printed with every carbon claim.

## Left out on purpose
- No dividers, no stat tiles, no new claims, no dated milestones (standing decisions).
- The Speed slide as a separate slide (folded into Properties).
- The carbon-sinks vision slide (removed earlier today, parked in archive/).
