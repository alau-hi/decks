Foundational scientific publication

All press coverage of Superwood traces back to a single peer-reviewed paper. Worth keeping handy when speaking with technical or academic audiences.

Song, J., Chen, C., Zhu, S. et al. "Processing bulk natural wood into a high-performance structural material." Nature 554, 224–228 (2018). DOI: 10.1038/nature25476 ↗

Co-authored by Liangbing Hu and 21 collaborators at the University of Maryland, including UMD's Center for Materials Innovation. This is the work that demonstrated the partial-delignification + hot-press densification process underlying SUPERWOOD.
